Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

ClipForge is a desktop app that runs only on its owner's computer. It has no servers and does not sell, share or collect personal data for anyone else.

What ClipForge accesses

  • TikTok: with your permission, your basic profile info (open ID, display name) and permission to post videos you choose to your account (scopes user.info.basic and video.publish).
  • YouTube: with your permission, the ability to upload videos you choose to your channel (scope youtube.upload).

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens, your upload queue and settings are stored only on your computer (in the app's folder in your user profile). Videos, titles, descriptions and hashtags are sent only to TikTok or YouTube, when you post them.

Sharing

No data is shared with anyone other than TikTok and Google, and only to carry out the posts you request.

Your choices

You can disconnect an account in ClipForge at any time, delete the app's folder to remove all stored data, and revoke access in your TikTok or Google account settings.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Barakahtags/clipforge-site/issues.
